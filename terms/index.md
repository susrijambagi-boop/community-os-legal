## What Community OS does

Community OS provides tools for:

- directional community-health analysis
- moderator recommendations
- recurring community programs
- contributor recognition

- weekly community-intelligence reports

Community OS is a moderation and community-operations aid. Its scores, rankings, recommendations, and reports are informational and should not be treated as authoritative Reddit analytics or as a substitute for moderator judgment.

Moderators should use contributor-recognition features carefully and should not treat them as definitive measures of a person's value, trustworthiness, identity, or status.

No automated system is guaranteed to operate without interruption or error. Moderators should review app behavior after enabling automation.
